Entry for make.fun's [news-image-20261118 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118) — "Sum up 18 November's world news in one image".

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` — a 1024×1024 front-page infographic
summing up Wednesday 18 November 2026 in six stories (Iran's drone strikes
on U.S. bases in Kuwait and Bahrain and the ADNOC ship hit in Hormuz, Trump
landing in Shenzhen for the Xi–Putin–Trump trilateral summit, Nvidia's
earnings beat that still saw its shares fall, Israel's new Gaza operation,
the Democrats flipping the Alaska Senate seat, and the midnight launch of
GTA VI) — to the output directory.
